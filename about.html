About Quorvan

A Workspace for Inference Routing

Quorvan helps infrastructure, MLOps, and performance teams manage model-serving endpoints, routing policies, and the records they use to judge latency, throughput, and reliability. The application keeps those records. It does not claim to run the model servers or the NVIDIA software teams use to serve them.

Tharunika Elanthirayan founded Quorvan on 30 April 2024. The company operates as Quorvan Digital (Pvt) Ltd in Sri Lanka and Quorvan Digital Inc. in the United States.
